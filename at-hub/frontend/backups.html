Database Backups

A full copy of the AT-HUB database — orders, POs, stock, invoices, users, everything — taken while it runs. Restore puts AT-HUB back exactly as it was at that moment; the current state is saved first as a pre-restore backup, so a restore can itself be undone. Attached files live in the uploads folder (not in the database) and aren't affected.
