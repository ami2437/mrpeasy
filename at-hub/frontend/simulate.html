What-if runs on a copy of the data. Nothing here changes real orders, stock or money.

Coming next

This module is set up (menu, page and its own permission on Users & Roles) and waiting for us to decide what it simulates. Some directions it could take:

  • Stock what-if: add a big order or a late PO and see which orders go short, and when.
  • Price / cost what-if: change a cost or a price and see the margin on open orders.
  • Cash flow: invoices due and vendor bills due over the next weeks, with "what if this customer pays late".
  • Practice mode: a full sandbox copy of AT-HUB to train new staff without touching live data.
